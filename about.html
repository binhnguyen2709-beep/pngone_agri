About PNG ONE

Building Vietnam's Bridge to Global Food Markets

PNG ONE Company Limited — Vietnamese legal name Công ty TNHH PNG ONE, trading as PNG ONE Co., Ltd — is an agricultural sourcing and export company dedicated to bringing Vietnam's finest rice, coffee, nuts, fruit, tea and spices to importers around the world.

Our Story

Rooted in Vietnam's Growing Regions

Vietnam is one of the world's leading producers of rice, coffee, cashew and pepper. PNG ONE was founded to connect that agricultural strength with international buyers who need a dependable, quality-focused sourcing partner — not just a trading middleman.

We work directly with growing regions and processing facilities across Vietnam, applying consistent quality control and transparent documentation to every shipment — from a single trial container to recurring long-term contracts.
