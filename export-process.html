Export Process

From Inquiry to Delivered Container

A clear, documented process so you always know what stage your order is at — and what to expect next.

1

Inquiry & Sample

Share your product, spec, and target volume. Samples arranged on request.

2

Quotation & Contract

FOB/CIF/CFR quotation issued within 24 hours; order confirmed by contract.

3

Production & QC

Sourcing, processing and multi-stage quality control begin.

4

Packaging & Labeling

Export-grade or private-label packaging, palletized and labeled.

5

Documentation

C/O, phytosanitary & fumigation certs, invoice, packing list, B/L.

6

Shipping & Delivery

Sea (FCL/LCL) or air freight, with tracking updates until arrival.

Incoterms We Support

EXWEx Works — buyer arranges pickup and freight
FOBFree On Board — most common for our shipments
CFRCost & Freight — freight included, insurance excluded
CIFCost, Insurance & Freight — door-to-port, all-in pricing

Payment Terms

  • T/T: 30% deposit on order confirmation, 70% balance against shipping documents
  • Irrevocable L/C at sight for larger recurring contracts
  • Terms confirmed in writing before production begins
