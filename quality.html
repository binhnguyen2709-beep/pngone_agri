Our Process

Multi-Stage Quality Control

1. Raw Material Inspection

Visual grading and moisture checks on intake.

2. Lab Testing

Residue, aflatoxin and microbiological testing as required.

3. In-Process QC

Line checks during cleaning, sorting, and packing.

4. Pre-Shipment Inspection

Final sample check against buyer specification before loading.

5. Third-Party Inspection

Independent inspection (e.g. SGS, Bureau Veritas, Vinacontrol) available on request.

Traceability

Every batch is linked back to its growing region and intake date, so any quality question can be traced to its source rather than treated as a one-off complaint.

Packaging & Food Safety

Fumigation on request, moisture-controlled storage, and food-grade packaging materials suited to sea, air, or reefer transport.
