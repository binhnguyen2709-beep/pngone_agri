Desiccated Coconut

A high-quality, long shelf-life product made from the white meat of mature fresh coconuts. After cleaning and preparation, the coconut meat is ground, shredded or sliced, then dried under strictly controlled conditions to reach optimal moisture. This modern drying process preserves the natural aroma, characteristic flavor and essential nutrients, delivering a versatile ingredient for the food industry worldwide.

  • Classified by fat content: high, medium, low fat
